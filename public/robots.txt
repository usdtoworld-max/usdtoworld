# A note on the two different kinds of "AI bot":
# - Training crawlers (GPTBot, CCBot, Bytespider, Google-Extended, and
#   others) bulk-scrape content to train models. Blocking these doesn't cost
#   this site any visibility in AI answer engines — it just opts out of
#   being training data. These are blocked via Cloudflare's Managed
#   robots.txt / AI Crawl Control (Security > AI Crawl Control in the
#   dashboard), which prepends its own Disallow rules for these at the edge,
#   so they're intentionally not repeated here (see the earlier note in this
#   file's git history — restating them just produced duplicate directives).
# - Answer/citation crawlers (ChatGPT-User, Claude-Web, PerplexityBot,
#   OAI-SearchBot) are different: they fetch a page in real time specifically
#   to answer a user's live query and cite this site as the source. Blocking
#   these doesn't protect against training — it just removes this site from
#   ever being cited by ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity, which is the opposite
#   of what GEO/AEO wants. They were blocked here previously; unblocked now
#   for exactly that reason.
